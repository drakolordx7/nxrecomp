# 441 Flat dark picture with the HUD intact: a draw that samples its own colour attachment

Symptom (sweep, S4g): a few areas (tallon_temple_hall, magmoor_south_core_tunnel and others) showed the HUD over a flat olive or black picture while the world was gone. The first screenshot of the same area was fine; the picture went flat a second or two later, together with the damage state of the HUD (orange corner pieces, health dropping).

Found with `@shot nvntrace_<name>` (traces the next whole frame): the HDR scene target held the full picture, the tone-mapped swap chain texture was fine after the first full-screen draw, and the picture became flat after a second full-screen draw (fragment shader f66ff695a8072e5c) that renders into the swap chain texture and samples the same texture (`fp_t_tcb_A -> t1`) next to the bloom texture. It is a pointwise read-modify-write: the console allows it, Vulkan does not (a feedback loop). The back end noticed the loop and bound the 1x1 dummy texture, so the draw wrote the bloom plus a constant.

Same class of bug, another place: the environment mip chain of 1024x512 HDR textures (eleven texture objects over one image, one per mip level) is built by full-screen draws that sample level k-1 while the pass renders level k. The objects are different `Texture` structs over one shared image, so the old test (same object) did not see the loop and left the image in the attachment layout while a descriptor expected shader-read-only (VUID-vkCmdDraw-imageLayout-00344, ten messages in one run).

Fix (`nvn_replay.cpp`: `is_color_feedback`, `feedback_prepare`, `feedback_clone`): a draw that samples the image of one of its colour attachments samples a copy of the whole image made just before the draw (the pass is ended, the image goes to transfer-source layout, every level and layer is copied into a private clone of the same shape, the clone is made shader-readable, the next pass loads the attachment again). A pointwise read sees the same values as on the hardware, and a read of another mip level sees that level. The test compares the images (the shared `TexGpu`), not the objects. One log line per draw (first 40): `nvn.replay: feedback: <texture> binding ... | attachment level ... | sampled view ...`.

Check: tallon_temple_hall after the damage state shows the room (`work/n4/th2/shots/th_c.png`), validation run clean (0 messages, `work/n4/th3`).

At scale: in the sweep of the build before this fix (`work/n4/full0`) six of 165 areas had a mostly black world under an intact HUD (tallon_temple_hall, chozo_antechamber, chozo_meditation_chamber, frigate_deck_beta_security_hall, chozo_reflecting_pool_access and magmoor_transport_tunnel_c: 21 to 201 fully black 64x64 cells of 510). A seventh, tallon_overgrown_cavern, shows the game-over screen in that run (the player died), which is not this bug. With the fix (`work/n4/m3full`) no area of the 165 has more than 12 fully black cells (the corners of dark rooms) (`work/n4/shots/before_black_world_tallon_temple_hall.png` and `after_black_world_tallon_temple_hall.png`).
