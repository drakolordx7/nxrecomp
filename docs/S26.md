# S26: playthrough part 2, the Frigate

Branch `play3` in `runtime`. The tools are in the root repository's `.weaver/` (`nav.py`, `frigate.py`, `rooms.py`, `survey.py`, `combat.py`, `route.py`, `live.py`). Screenshots and the route log are in `work/run_play3/route/` (not committed, they come from the game).

## Short answer

The scripted run now goes from New Game through both hangar force fields, along the walkway, through the corridor door, the Air Lock Access (with its scan puzzle), the Deck Alpha Access Hall and into the Emergency Evacuation Area, all with real input and no warps. It stops in the Emergency Evacuation Area: the room is a burning wreck and every crossing attempt ended in the fire. The Parasite Queen, the escape, Tallon IV and the first Missile were not reached. Nothing here needed a warp except development: `@warp` was used to jump into a room to work on it, and to survey the whole Frigate.

The S25 "stuck on the walkway" problem was not a navigation problem. The lights of the second force field were never hit: the lock-on in the old `shoot` helper re-aims at another target, so the field stayed up and the player stood against an invisible wall. With the lights shot without lock-on the field drops and the walkway opens.

## What was built

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `@rays IN OUT` (script step) | Batch of ray casts through the game's own `NCollisionMP1::RayStaticIntersection`. 23,000 rays take 0.3 s. A grid of down rays gives every walkable floor of the loaded areas, with head room from up rays. |
| `@probe IN OUT` | Puts the player's own collision box (`CPlayerMP1::GetCollidableBox`) at positions and asks the static world whether it collides. This replaced most of the ray checks of an edge: it sees lips, rails and rubble the way the game does. |
| `@peek IN OUT`, `@poke IN` | Read and write guest memory, following pointer chains. Used to follow enemies and doors without scanning memory again, and (development only) to restore health. |
| `@objs *` | Lists every live object by class name (the class table comes from the IDA database, `NXRT_TEST_VTABLES`). The scan is ten times faster now (a range check before the lookup). Docks also print the area they lead to and their plane. |
| `nav.py` | Scan, A* over floor nodes (walk, drop and jump edges), edge checks, walking with stall handling, hops over rails, feeling along a wall when stuck. |
| `survey.py`, `rooms.py` | Warps into every Frigate area once, saves docks and a height map of each, and builds the room graph from docks that sit at the same place. `rooms.py frigate A B` gives the shortest room path. |
| `combat.py` | Finds enemies, aims by mouse counts (0.000768 rad per count, exact), fires, reads the damage volumes (`CScriptTriggerMP1` at +528) and door states (`CScriptDoorMP1` word at +1036, bits 1 and 2 set = open). |
| `frigate.py cross NEXT` / `run TARGET` | Finds the dock to the next room, walks to the door, shoots it, waits for the door state, walks through. `run` repeats this room by room along the room graph. |

The room graph of the Frigate (28 areas) is complete in `work/run_play3/survey/frigate/`. Shortest route from the hangar to the Reactor Core: Hangar, Air Lock, Deck Alpha Access Hall, Emergency Evacuation Area, Deck Alpha Umbilical Hall, Map Facility, Connection Elevator to Deck Beta 2, Deck Beta Conduit Hall, Biotech Research Area 1, Connection Elevator to Deck Beta, Deck Gamma Monitor Hall, Reactor Core. The Air Lock also connects to the Deck Alpha Mech Shaft, but that door stays locked from the Air Lock side.

## What was learned about the rooms

* The player stands on a box, so jumping and walking are checked against a box, not a point.
* Samus jumps 3.5 units high (a 0.4 s button or a tap, the hold length hardly matters) and walks 6.8 units per second. A rail 1.5 high at the edge needs the jump to start in place and the run to follow; `nav.py` plans that ("rise for tau seconds, then run").
* Air Lock Access: the exit door answers "cannot be opened until the room is repressurized". The panel is a scan point that only becomes scannable about 50 s after arrival. Scanning it earlier does nothing. After the scan the door opens to one shot.
* Doors keep their open state in the low bits of a word; the route code reads it instead of guessing.
* Emergency Evacuation Area: six damage volumes cover the middle of the room, 0.2 damage per tick (about 10 to 12 health per second). They are listed by `combat.damage_triggers()`. A height map (`colmap.py`) shows a big flat floor (y = 0) south of the catwalk, a hill with wreckage in the middle and a tall diagonal beam wall south of the hill. The way to the exit door at the south end crosses the hill or runs past it on the east side; every attempt either stalled at a lip or in the fire.

## Where the route stops, and why

All crossing attempts of the Emergency Evacuation Area ended the same way: the planner reaches the flat floor in front of the hill, then a lip and a tall wall make it stall, the fire wins the next few seconds. What was tried: edge checks with rays (too strict on rubble, they blocked every way), with the player's box (accurate, but the planner then found no valid path in the room), hazard costs for the fire volumes, route hints by hand, a straight dash through the fire (reached the middle twice, died there once). What would finish it: the exact hop and lip geometry at the south end of the hill checked with `@probe` in a fine grid (0.25), or a small one-off script for this room. The health guard (`HPGUARD`) now stops a script below 25 health so a room can be retried after `combat.heal`.

## Bugs and findings

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| S25 route stuck at the first ledge | lock-on in the `shoot` helper missed the force field lights (field 2 stayed up) | `route.py` and `nav.fire_at` aim without lock-on and fire two shots |
| Air Lock door never opened | the panel scan point appears about 50 s after arrival, and an early scan is wasted | `frigate.cross` waits and scans again every other attempt |
| `@objs` took 35 s | a lookup for every 8 bytes of 8.5 GB | range check first (about 3 s) |
| A failing `@probe` ended the whole run | a step failure ends the run | the step answers "collides" for every line and logs why |

The game itself did not crash and drew nothing wrong in the rooms visited. Nothing in the runtime needed fixing beyond the test steps above.

## New Game that never reached the first area (S25)

The thread dump of that run (`work/run_play2/hang1_stacks.txt`, 78 threads) shows the main guest thread parked in the live-script runner, sleeping in `poll_live`, and every other thread idle or blocked in the emulated kernel, with no host deadlock. In other words the script had run out of steps while the area was still unset, so the game stood still by design and the load never finished. That fits a script that stopped too early (the `newgame` helper waits for `igt >= 1`, which a stale value can satisfy) and not a runtime hang. It was not reproduced: this stream started the game about a dozen times and every New Game reached the hangar in 100 to 190 s, some of them with two games running. No fix was needed; the `newgame` helper should check the area as well (not done).

## Morph ball speed (S25: +1.0 to +2.2 % at 120 to 240)

Measured with 10 repeats per rate in one process, twice per rate, in both orders (`.weaver/phys_ball_rep.py`, results in `work/run_play3_ball/ball_results.json`; two games ran at the same time on the machine):

| Steps per second | steady speed (units/s) | against 60 |
|---|---|---|
| 60 | 16.220 (first process), 16.336 (second) | |
| 120 | 16.493 (both processes) | +0.96 to +1.69 % |
| 144 | 16.378 (both) | +0.26 to +0.98 % |
| 240 | 16.383 (both) | +0.29 to +1.01 % |

The ten repeats inside a process are identical to four digits, so the run-to-run noise is between processes, and only at 60 (two levels, 0.7 % apart). At 120, 144 and 240 the value is the same in both processes. So the difference is real and repeatable, between +0.3 % and +1.7 % depending on which 60 value is the reference. It is not monotonic (120 is faster than 144 and 240), which a plain integration error of the step size would be. The mechanism was not found: the ball's rolling force code (`CMorphBallMP1::ComputeMarioMovement`) is a long function that combines forces and contact state, and a flat test area with a long straight roll was not run. Not fixed.

## Decisions

* Navigation reads the game's own collision (rays and the player's box) instead of parsing area files or recording inputs in Eden.
* Normal difficulty, pad input, as in S25.
* `@warp` only for development and the survey; the Frigate part of the route above is played.
* The probe-based edge check is on by default (`NAVRAYS=1` returns to the ray checks).
