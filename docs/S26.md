# S26: playthrough part 2, the Frigate

Branch `play3` in `runtime`. The tools live in the root repository's `.weaver/` (`nav.py`, `frigate.py`, `rooms.py`, `survey.py`, `combat.py`, `route.py`, `live.py`). Screenshots and the route log are in `work/run_play3/route/` (not committed, they come from the game).

## Short answer

The scripted run now goes from New Game through both hangar force fields, along the walkway, through the corridor door, the Air Lock Access (with its scan puzzle), the Deck Alpha Access Hall and into the Emergency Evacuation Area. It uses real input and no warps. It stops in the Emergency Evacuation Area, which is a burning wreck, and every crossing attempt ended in the fire. The Parasite Queen, the escape, Tallon IV and the first Missile were not reached. Warps were used only during development: `@warp` jumped into a room to work on it, and to survey the whole Frigate.

The S25 "stuck on the walkway" problem was a combat problem, not a navigation one. The lights of the second force field were never hit. The lock-on in the old `shoot` helper re-aims at another target, so the field stayed up and the player stood against an invisible wall. Shooting the lights without lock-on drops the field and opens the walkway.

## What was built

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `@rays IN OUT` (script step) | Runs a batch of ray casts through the game's own `NCollisionMP1::RayStaticIntersection`. 23,000 rays take 0.3 s. A grid of down rays finds every walkable floor in the loaded areas, and up rays measure the head room. |
| `@probe IN OUT` | Places the player's own collision box (`CPlayerMP1::GetCollidableBox`) at given positions and asks the static world whether it collides. It replaced most of the ray checks for an edge, and it sees lips, rails and rubble the way the game does. |
| `@peek IN OUT`, `@poke IN` | Read and write guest memory, following pointer chains. Used to track enemies and doors without scanning memory again, and, during development only, to restore health. |
| `@objs *` | Lists every live object by class name. The class table comes from the IDA database, `NXRT_TEST_VTABLES`. The scan is now ten times faster because a range check runs before the lookup. Docks also print the area they lead to and their plane. |
| `nav.py` | Scanning, A* search over floor nodes (walk, drop and jump edges), edge checks, walking with stall handling, hops over rails, and feeling along a wall when stuck. |
| `survey.py`, `rooms.py` | Warps into each Frigate area once, saves its docks and a height map, and builds the room graph from docks that sit at the same place. `rooms.py frigate A B` prints the shortest room path. |
| `combat.py` | Finds enemies, aims using mouse counts (0.000768 rad per count, exact), fires, and reads the damage volumes (`CScriptTriggerMP1` at +528) and door states (`CScriptDoorMP1` word at +1036; bits 1 and 2 set means open). |
| `frigate.py cross NEXT` / `run TARGET` | Finds the dock to the next room, walks to the door, shoots it, waits for the door state, and walks through. `run` repeats this room by room along the room graph. |

The Frigate room graph (28 areas) is complete in `work/run_play3/survey/frigate/`. The shortest route from the hangar to the Reactor Core runs: Hangar, Air Lock, Deck Alpha Access Hall, Emergency Evacuation Area, Deck Alpha Umbilical Hall, Map Facility, Connection Elevator to Deck Beta 2, Deck Beta Conduit Hall, Biotech Research Area 1, Connection Elevator to Deck Beta, Deck Gamma Monitor Hall, Reactor Core. The Air Lock also connects to the Deck Alpha Mech Shaft, but that door stays locked from the Air Lock side.

## What was learned about the rooms

* The player stands on a box, so jumping and walking are checked against a box, not a point.
* Samus jumps 3.5 units high (a 0.4 s button or a tap; the hold length hardly matters) and walks 6.8 units per second. A rail 1.5 high at the edge needs the jump to start in place, with the run following. `nav.py` plans that as "rise for tau seconds, then run".
* Air Lock Access: the exit door answers "cannot be opened until the room is repressurized". The panel is a scan point that only becomes scannable about 50 s after arrival. Scanning it earlier does nothing. After the scan, one shot opens the door.
* Doors keep their open state in the low bits of a word. The route code reads that state instead of guessing.
* Emergency Evacuation Area: six damage volumes cover the middle of the room, 0.2 damage per tick (about 10 to 12 health per second). They are listed by `combat.damage_triggers()`. A height map (`colmap.py`) shows a big flat floor (y = 0) south of the catwalk, a hill with wreckage in the middle, and a tall diagonal beam wall south of the hill. The way to the exit door at the south end either crosses the hill or runs past it on the east side. Every attempt stalled at a lip or ended in the fire.

## Where the route stops, and why

Every crossing attempt in the Emergency Evacuation Area failed the same way. The planner reaches the flat floor in front of the hill, then a lip and a tall wall stall it, and the fire takes the next few seconds. Tried so far:

* Edge checks with rays: too strict on rubble, they blocked every route.
* Edge checks with the player's box: accurate, but the planner then found no valid path in the room.
* Hazard costs for the fire volumes.
* Route hints by hand.
* A straight dash through the fire: reached the middle twice, died there once.

To finish it, check the exact hop and lip geometry at the south end of the hill with `@probe` on a fine grid (0.25), or write a small one-off script for this room. The health guard (`HPGUARD`) now stops a script below 25 health, so a room can be retried after `combat.heal`.

## Bugs and findings

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| S25 route stuck at the first ledge | lock-on in the `shoot` helper missed the force field lights (field 2 stayed up) | `route.py` and `nav.fire_at` aim without lock-on and fire two shots |
| Air Lock door never opened | the panel scan point appears about 50 s after arrival, and an early scan is wasted | `frigate.cross` waits and scans again every other attempt |
| `@objs` took 35 s | a lookup for every 8 bytes of 8.5 GB | range check first (about 3 s) |
| A failing `@probe` ended the whole run | a step failure ends the run | the step answers "collides" for every line and logs why |

The game did not crash, and nothing looked wrong in the rooms visited. Apart from the test steps above, the runtime needed no fixes.

## New Game that never reached the first area (S25)

The thread dump from that run (`work/run_play2/hang1_stacks.txt`, 78 threads) shows the main guest thread parked in the live-script runner, sleeping in `poll_live`. Every other thread was idle or blocked in the emulated kernel, with no host deadlock. So the script had run out of steps while the area was still unset. The game stood still by design, and the load never finished. This points to a script that stopped too early (the `newgame` helper waits for `igt >= 1`, which a stale value can satisfy), not to a runtime hang.

It was not reproduced. This session started the game about a dozen times, and every New Game reached the hangar in 100 to 190 s, some with two games running at once. No fix was made. The `newgame` helper should also check the area; that is not done yet.

## Morph ball speed (S25: +1.0 to +2.2 % at 120 to 240)

Measured with 10 repeats per rate in one process, run twice per rate in both orders (`.weaver/phys_ball_rep.py`; results in `work/run_play3_ball/ball_results.json`). Two games ran at the same time on the machine.

| Steps per second | steady speed (units/s) | against 60 |
|---|---|---|
| 60 | 16.220 (first process), 16.336 (second) | |
| 120 | 16.493 (both processes) | +0.96 to +1.69 % |
| 144 | 16.378 (both) | +0.26 to +0.98 % |
| 240 | 16.383 (both) | +0.29 to +1.01 % |

The ten repeats inside one process match to four digits. So the run-to-run noise is between processes, and it shows only at 60 (two levels, 0.7 % apart). At 120, 144 and 240 the value is the same in both processes, so the difference is real and repeatable: between +0.3 % and +1.7 %, depending on which 60 value is the reference. It is not monotonic (120 is faster than 144 and 240), which a plain integration error of the step size would not produce.

The mechanism is not known. The ball's rolling force code (`CMorphBallMP1::ComputeMarioMovement`) is a long function that combines forces and contact state. A flat test area with a long straight roll was not run. Not fixed.

## Audio

One run used the real sink (XAudio2 on the default device, output volume 0, with every frame also written to a WAV file): New Game, the opening cutscene and 200 s of game time, 274 s of wall time, with the real-time clock and no `@rate`. Results come from `.weaver/audio_run.py` and `.weaver/audio_analyse.py`, with output in `work/run_play3_audio/out/`:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Sink counters at close | written 13,045,200 frames, accepted 13,045,200, dropped 0, underruns 0, queue peak 10 buffers |
| WAV | 48 kHz stereo, 271.8 s of audio, no stretch of exact silence over 100 ms inside active audio, no click above 16384 (largest jump 3852) |
| Against the clock | audio length 271.8 s for 273.8 s of wall time (0.992); the game clock moved 202 s in the same run (loads and menus stand still), so the audio is not tied to game time and drifts from it by design |

Caveats: the movie part is short. The title loop played about once, 8 s, and the menu movies were not covered. The WAV header length is stale, so use the data length. The log holds 68 limiter warnings (frames woke 40 to 170 ms late). Nobody has listened to the output. A first attempt at the same run was cut off at 29 s by something outside the game; the log shows no crash.

## Decisions

* Navigation reads the game's own collision, through rays and the player's box, rather than parsing area files or recording inputs in Eden.
* Normal difficulty, pad input, as in S25.
* `@warp` is for development and the survey only. The Frigate part of the route above is played without warps.
* The probe-based edge check is on by default (`NAVRAYS=1` returns to the ray checks).
