# S44: glow colour

One optional setting, off by default, so the game looks as it does now:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `[tweaks] glow_color` | `off` | `R,G,B` (0 to 255 each): the glows of Samus's equipment (suit, morph ball, arm cannon) take this colour. |
| `[tweaks] suit_glow` | `auto` | `auto` follows `glow_color`, `off` keeps the game's colour, `R,G,B` is the suit's own colour. |
| `[tweaks] ball_glow` | `auto` | The same for the morph ball. |
| `[tweaks] gun_glow` | `auto` | The same for the arm cannon (its lit panels and the charge glow). |

The launcher has a Tweaks tab, and so has the F1 page: a switch, three sliders with a colour swatch, a Reset button and a "Per part" choice. The F1 page shows the colour in the game while the sliders move; Apply and save keeps it. Every change applies at once, with no restart. The keys are in `docs/CONFIG.md`.

## Why the first version did not show

The first version recoloured the morph ball's colour tables and gave the ball's inner glow and the charge effect a particle modulation colour. Its screenshots kept the game's colours, because what the eye reads as "the glow" is neither of those:

* The bright white-blue stripes of the morph ball, the orange lit panels of the arm cannon and the suit's lights are the models' incandescence: the materials' `ICAN` glow map times their `ICNC` colour, drawn with the model.
* The colour tables only feed the trail, the boost glow, the ball's light and the morph flash, which are faint or only there while rolling or boosting.

## What changes the colour now

Model incandescence (the main glow). `CModelFlagsMPT` (the flags every model draw takes) has a colour mode for the glow: mode 2, built by `CModelFlagsMPT::ColorModulateIncan(CColor4f const&)` (0xb41578). `CModelFlagsMPT::CreateModelRenderState` (0xb42074) copies a mode-2 colour into the model render state's incandescence modulation (render state +40, flag bit 2). The game uses it for `CScriptColorModulateMP1`. Samus's models are drawn with `Normal` flags (mode 0, white). The tweak hooks the three model draw functions they go through and, for the calls that draw Samus's equipment, passes a copy of the flags in mode 2 with the setting's colour (linear):

| Hook site (new, `recomp/games/mpr/main.sites`) | Flags in |
|---|---|
| 0xfa9694 `CModelDataMP1::Render(CGameRenderCommandState&, CStateManager const&, CTransform4f const&, CModelFlagsMPT const&, SCameraData const*)` | x3 |
| 0xfa6c0c `CModelDataMP1::Render(CGameRenderCommandState&, EWhichModel, ...)` | x4 |
| 0xfa7dd0 `CModelDataMP1::RenderGun(CGameRenderCommandState&, EWhichModel, CTransform4f const&, SGunRenderParams const&, CModelFlagsMPT const&)` | x5 |

The caller is told apart by the return address (x30 at the entry; the lifted code sets it before every call, and the wrapper's tail call keeps it):

* Ball: `CMorphBallMP1::Render` hull 0xcea890 and Spider Ball 0xceade4.
* Gun (S44 only): `CGunWeaponMP1::Draw` 0xca2594/0xca2638/0xca26d4, `DrawGunWithRain` 0xca2a24, `CPlayerGunMP1::Render` 0xc92a48/0xc92b68.
* Suit (S44 only): `CPlayerMP1::Render` 0xc72cd8/0xc731e4/0xc7340c/0xc72d9c/0xc72ff8/0xc730a4 and `CSamusDollMP1::Draw` 0xe7b00c.

Since S44b only the ball's two callers got mode 2, and since S44c none: the hooks are no longer registered (see S44c). The cannon and the suit are recoloured through their glow textures instead (next section). The hook sites stay in `main.sites`, so no relift was needed.

Only mode 0 and mode 2 flags are changed. Additive, colour-add (the boost glow) and darken draws keep the game's effect. The flags copy is written to the guest stack below the caller's frame, and the draw function runs with sp below it. The render commands copy the flags (`CNormalModelRenderCommandMP1`, `CGunRenderCommandMP1` constructors), so nothing keeps the pointer.

Colour tables. `CMorphBallMP1` has five static tables of linear `CColor4f`, five entries each (glow index 0 Power, 1 Varia, 2 Varia with the Spider Ball, 3 Gravity, 4 Phazon; `CMorphBallMP1 + 0xc` is the index), filled by the module's static init:

* `skBallNormalSwooshColors` 0x4b24a48
* `skBallBoostedSwooshColors` 0x4b24a98 (trail)
* `skBallBoostedHullGlowColors` 0x4b24ae8 (boost glow)
* `skBallLightningSwooshColors` 0x4b24b38
* `skBallLightColors` 0x4b24b88 (light, morph flash, pause screen)

They are rewritten in place at the frame boundary when the wanted colour changes (each entry: the setting's hue at the game's brightest channel, alpha kept). The game's values are written back when the setting goes off.

Particle modulation. The ball's inner glow (`CMorphBallMP1 + 0x2100 + 8 * index`, a `CElementGen` drawn with no modulation colour) and the arm cannon's charge glow get the colour through `CParticleGen::SetModulationColor` (vtable `+184`). It is called on the guest every frame while the setting is on, from the existing `CPlayerMP1::ProcessInput` and `CPlayerGunMP1::Update` hook sites. For the charge glow, `CGunWeaponMP1::DrawMuzzleFx` draws the generator at `CGunWeaponMP1 + 1392 + 16 * stage` while the charge stage at `CGunWeaponMP1 + 1520` is 1 or 2; the weapon is at `CPlayerGunMP1 + 1272`.

## S44b: the cannon's lit panels and the suit's lights

The NVN trace (`@shot nvntrace_<name>`, `docs/S4e.md`) of a first-person frame, together with the decompiled shaders (`spirv-cross` on the shader cache), shows where these colours come from. RenderDoc cannot capture the game.

* **Arm cannon panels.** The barrel draws with fragment shader `102960b5d01faefd` (and `64223e9717113568` for other parts) at depth 0.0098..0.039. The glow is `ramp.rgb * mask * c5[2].x * c4[0].z`. The mask is a grey 1024 x 1024 texture of the panels' stripes. The colour comes from a 128 x 16 ASTC 8x5 sRGB "ramp" texture, read at a second UV set. The intensities are scalars. The shader has no ICNC term, which is why mode 2 never reached the panels. Every beam uses the same ramp. The beams' own effects (128 x 4 ramps, 512 x 512 textures, particles) are separate and keep their colours. The same shader also samples a second, orange 128 x 128 ramp on some parts.
* **Suit lights.** The suit body draws with fragment shader `3065a1ce7022c16a` (vertex shader `bc5522ba683434cf`) and the first-person arm with `8f2ff87c2b6f057d`. Their emission is `ICAN.rgb * c5[2].rgb + c5[3]`, and for the arm it is multiplied by `c5[0].x * c4[0].z`. `c5[2]` is `(400, 400, 400)` in the trace, so the colour is in the glow map: green, 2048 x 2048 for the body and 2048 x 1024 for the arm. Mode 2 would replace that ICNC, but the suit is drawn in mode 6 (alpha blended) and mode 8 (additive) during the morph, and mode 2 replaces those modes.

**Fix: the textures are recoloured on upload.** The glow ramps and glow maps are recoloured as the game uploads them, through the runtime's texture edit hook (`nxrt/tex_edit.h`, the S45 mechanism). That hook has one owner, so `games/mpr/mpr_texedit.{h,cpp}` holds it and hands each texture to the tint (S45) or the glow, whichever claims it. Each texel keeps its brightness and takes the setting's hue (`glow_recolor_texel`: the brightest channel in linear light becomes the setting's colour scaled to it; alpha is kept). This **replaces** the hue of every beam's panel glow, because all the beams share the ramp. The colour applies at once (debounced 100 ms while the F1 sliders move). The four textures are rebuilt in the background in about 0.8 to 1.0 s, then uploaded again. With the setting off they go back to the game's data.

**Texture identity.** The glow tells its textures apart by a hash of the last quarter of the tiled storage (the smaller mip levels), salted with the size, shape and storage size (`glow_texture_tail_hash`). A hash of the whole storage did not work: the game streams a glow map's level 0 in after its smaller levels, so the first upload of the suit's glow map has an empty level 0 and a different hash. The tail stays the same. To find the textures, `NXRT_GLOW_LOG_TEXTURES=WxH,...` logs every sRGB ASTC texture's tail with a discovery id and claims those sizes under that id without changing them. The runtime's line `texture N: edited by the host (id ...)` then names the texture id, and a trace shows which draw samples it.

| Texture (ASTC 8x5 sRGB) | Tail hash | Sampled by |
|---|---|---|
| 128 x 16, 8 levels: cannon panel ramp | `e6ef56252d1ac680` | `102960b5d01faefd`, `64223e9717113568` |
| 128 x 128, 8 levels: orange cannon ramp | `095d5c0d4843b57f` | `102960b5d01faefd` |
| 2048 x 2048, 12 levels: Varia Suit glow map (S44b called it the Power Suit's) | `8dbb31ab811c3e94` | `3065a1ce7022c16a` |
| 2048 x 1024, 12 levels: Varia Suit arm glow map | `2d9b3b57fa799f2a` | `8f2ff87c2b6f057d` |

Shader `3065a1ce7022c16a` also samples a 1024 x 1024 glow texture (tail `315648d5a9d07890`). It is not in the table because the hangar's red and green signal lights share it: with it, they turned magenta too.

**The ball's shell tint (not changed, inference).** With mode 2 the ball's hull switches from instanced shader `f6b9c653dc752fb7` to `02fe1ae2e1b12445`. In that variant the ICNC lands in `c4[1]`, which is black in vanilla, and acts as emission over the whole hull. That is why the whole shell turns pink. In vanilla the stripes take their colour from per-instance flat inputs, which the model flags cannot reach. Fixing this needs the ball's own glow textures or instance data. It was not done.

## S44c: the suits' lights, the ball's shell, the other suits

**Why the suit seemed unchanged.** Three things, each checked in game (`glow4_run.py`, headless, muted, 720p, pool copies off):

* The suit's lights are faint by design. In the suit body draws (fragment shader `3065a1ce7022c16a`, vertex shader `bc5522ba683434cf`) `c5[2]` is `(1, 1, 1)` and `c5[3]` is 0, so the glow is the glow map as it is. The `(400, 400, 400)` that S44b quoted belongs to another draw of the same shader with vertex shader `347f1aa409f61d7b`, the 1024 x 1024 glow texture of the hangar's signal lights. The suit's glow maps are mostly black: 5 % (Varia) to 11 % (Phazon) of the texels are lit, small lights on the visor, chest, belt, knees and shins. From behind, during the morph, almost none of them show.
* The wrong copy was edited first. The game uploads a suit glow map before its level 0 is streamed in. That early copy has the same tail hash, and its level 0 decodes to garbage (95 % of the texels lit at full strength). The texture edit hook keeps edited data per id, so the complete texture got the data made from the early copy. That was the strong magenta seen at times on the back of the suit, an artefact. A live switch made the right data, but from behind nothing showed. Fix: the glow's edit id is now the table row plus a hash of the whole storage (`glow_texture_id`), so the complete texture gets its own edit. The log shows each suit map edited twice at load, first the early copy, then the complete one (`level 0: 222980 of 4194304 texels lit`).
* The names were wrong. Samus wears the Varia Suit on the Frigate, and S44b's two "Power Suit" maps are the Varia Suit's (asset ids `9758f9d7`, `834be0bf`).

The slot was right: the trace of a morph frame shows the suit draws sampling the edited texture in the shader's glow-map slot (binding 132), the same texture id that the `edited by the host` line names.

**Proof, from the front.** The pause screen's Inventory shows Samus in full (MINUS, then X). With magenta, every light of the suit turns magenta: visor glow, chest, belt, knees, shins (`work/shots_wip/glow4_before_suit_front.png` / `glow4_after_suit_front.png`). The suit's paint keeps its colours.

**The other suits.** Their glow maps were found from the suit models' texture slots (`tint_survey.py slots`: Power `913c579b`, Varia `2aed633c`, Gravity `5d4b52e2`, Phazon `e46a4fa2` in DuplicateData.pak, the first-person arms in SamusGunMP1.pak). The tail hash of each asset's own tiled buffer equals the game's: the game's storage has the same size, and all eight tails appear in the `NXRT_GLOW_LOG_TEXTURES` log of the hangar, where every suit is loaded. To see another suit on the pause screen, the test sets the player state's current suit (`CPlayerStateMP1 + 0x24`: 0 Power, 1 Gravity, 2 Varia, 3 Phazon; `+0x28` is the item count, `+0x2c` the item table) with `@poke`, as well as giving the item. `@give` alone writes only the item table.

Checked in game: Varia, Gravity (cyan lights to magenta, `glow4_*_gravity_front.png`) and Phazon (red lights and veins to magenta, `glow4_*_phazon_front.png`). The Power Suit's map is matched and edited in the same runs, but the Power Suit was not looked at on screen. Not in the table: the Gravity Suit's second glow texture (`7f5c390c`, 8 x 8, the cyan stripes on top of its shoulders, which stay cyan) and the Phazon Suit's 128 x 4 glow ramp (`b9513805`). Neither was found among the game's uploads with the asset's tail. The face's grey ICAN (`90766316`) is left alone. The 1024 x 1024 hangar texture stays out (a test checks it).

**The ball's shell.** Without mode 2, the ball's stripes still turn magenta and the shell keeps the game's colours (`glow4_before_ball.png` / `glow4_after_ball.png`). The stripes are the ball's inner glow, a particle effect tinted through its modulation colour, and its light (`skBallLightColors`). In both hull shaders (`f6b9c653dc752fb7`, and `02fe1ae2e1b12445` in mode 2) the emission is one constant for the whole hull (`c4[0].y * c5[1].rgb + c4[1].rgb`). There is no glow texture in the hull draw, so mode 2 could only light the whole shell. The three model draw hooks are no longer registered. Their sites stay in `main.sites` (no relift).

**Test aids.** `NXRT_GLOW_TEST_FILL=1` paints every glow texture texel in the colour at full strength (shows whether a texture reaches the screen). Each edit logs how many texels of level 0 are lit and the peak. Discovery ids (`NXRT_GLOW_LOG_TEXTURES=WxH,...`) now have bit 55 set, so they never look like a table row.

## Colour math

The setting is sRGB; incandescence and table colours are linear. The incandescence colour is `linear(setting)` and multiplies the glow map, so a white glow (the ball's stripes) takes the colour exactly and a coloured one mixes with it. The particle glows are multiplied by the setting's sRGB bytes, so the yellow charge times magenta gives red-pink. A darker setting gives a dimmer glow.

## Results

One live run of the Frigate hangar (`glow2_44.py`, headless, muted), with pairs from the same spot and pose: the game's colours, then `glow_color = 255,0,255` switched live through `NXRT_GLOW_TEST_FILE`. The log's per-site counts (`draw sites while on`) show which draws were changed.

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| Morph ball, standing and rolling | S44c: changes; the stripes turn magenta and the shell keeps its colours (inner glow and ball light; mode 2 is gone, see S44c). S44: the white-blue stripes turned magenta (model incandescence, site 0xcea890, flags mode 0 to 2) and the shell turned pink. Whether that comes from the incandescence or from the ball's light (`skBallLightColors`, now magenta) was not separated. |
| Arm cannon charge | Changes. The charge ball and its rays go from yellow to pink-red (particle modulation of the charge generator at `CGunWeaponMP1 + 1392 + 16 * stage`). The first version read +1416/+1424 as effect modules, which only matched by chance, so its tint never reached the charge. A yellow particle times magenta is red, so the colour mixes with the game's yellow instead of replacing it: particle modulation is a byte colour and cannot go above the game's own. |
| Arm cannon's lit panels | S44: not changed (mode 2 on the cannon draws left the panels yellow). S44b: changes. The yellow panel stripes turn magenta, idle and charged (`work/shots_wip/glow3_before_cannon.png` / `glow3_after_cannon.png`, `glow3_*_charge.png`), through the recoloured ramp. The hangar around the cannon keeps its colours. |
| Suit | S44c: changes. Seen from the front on the pause screen, the lights of the Varia, Gravity and Phazon Suits turn magenta (see S44c). S44b: the Power Suit's body and arm glow maps are recoloured and uploaded again; the log names each one (`glow: Power Suit glow map: recoloured`, `texture N: edited by the host`). No magenta is visible in the views checked (`glow3_*_suit8.png`, `glow3_*_suit16.png`: Samus from the front, 8 and 16 frames into the morph): the lit parts of the glow map do not show in these poses and this light. Not confirmed on screen. |

## Not done

* The Gravity Suit's shoulder stripes (`7f5c390c`) and the Phazon Suit's glow ramp (`b9513805`): not found in the game's uploads (S44c).
* The Power Suit's glow on screen (its maps are matched and edited, but it was not looked at; Samus first wears it after the Frigate).
* A true colour replacement for the charge (it mixes with the game's yellow).

* `games/mpr/play_glow.h`, `play_glow.cpp` (hooks, tables, particles, glow textures), `play_glow_ui.cpp` (F1 Tweaks tab), `mpr_texedit.{h,cpp}` (the texture edit hook shared with the tint, S44b). Moved from `src/gfx/play` with the game boundary.
* Shared, small: `play_types.h` (`Settings::tweaks`), `play_config.cpp` (`[tweaks]`), `play_defaults.cpp` (factory file), `play.cpp` (install, settings, frame, ProcessInput), `play_ui.cpp` (the tab), `apps/launcher/page_tweaks.cpp`, `pages.h`, `main.cpp`, `cmake/launcher.cmake`.
