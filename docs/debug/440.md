# 440 Colour noise over whole rooms: texture objects over one storage had separate images

Symptom (sweep, S15; S4e hangar door): rectangles, in some rooms the whole picture, of saturated colour noise that follows the shapes of the room (a chain-link wall in chozo_watery_hall_access: 2,398 noisy 16x16 blocks, tallon_biohazard_containment 2,175, chozo_totem_access 1,962; 20 areas in all, `.weaver/noisescore.py`). The noise depended on what had been loaded before: the same room alone was clean, the same room as the third area of a warp chain was not.

Mechanism: the game makes several texture objects over one piece of memory, one per mip level of the environment cube map it renders, and another over the same bytes that the lighting shader samples. The old back end gave each object its own Vulkan image. The sampled image never received the mips that the others rendered; it was filled from the stale bytes of the game memory (whatever textures had lived at that address before), so the room was lit with garbage reflections. That is why it depended on the load history. The fix is the sharing of one image per storage and shape (`texture_registry_find_alias`, S4f, commit b1c0e94). Sparse volumes (6df0bb9) and integer attributes (948b9c9) are separate fixes.

How it was pinned down (`work/n4/e0..e3`, the chozo chain vault_access, watery_hall, watery_hall_access, noise blocks of the two shots per area):

| build | alias sharing | sparse tiles | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| the exe of the sweep branch (game-build-sweep, built 08:48) | no | no | noise in 2 of 3 areas (582 and 567 blocks) |
| current main, `NXRT_BIS_NO_ALIAS=1 NXRT_BIS_NO_SPARSE=1` | no | no | noise in 2 of 3 areas (649, 245) |
| current main, `NXRT_BIS_NO_ALIAS=1` | no | yes | noise in 1 of 3 (753, 311) |
| current main, `NXRT_BIS_NO_SPARSE=1` | yes | no | clean (0, 0, 0) |
| current main | yes | yes | clean (0 blocks in all 165 areas of frigate, tallon, chozo, magmoor) |

(The two switches exist only in a scratch worktree, not in the branch.) The six "texture N: not uploaded (no storage)" lines that every area logged before were the sparse probe volumes (S4f), they made lit dynamic objects black but did not make noise.

Rechecked on the merged build (main plus nvn4, mpr.exe of `work/n4/exe_m3`): all 275 areas of the sweep were reached, 275 OK, and no area has a noise block (`work/n4/m3full`, 165 areas in 98 minutes, and `work/n4/m3full2`, 110 areas in 77 minutes; `tools/test/sweep.py --exe ... --worlds ...`, scored with `.weaver/n4noise.py`). The three worst areas of the first sweep, tallon_biohazard_containment, chozo_watery_hall_access and chozo_totem_access, show 0 blocks (2,175, 2,398 and 1,962 before). The hangar door of the opening scene is clean as well (`work/n4/shots/after_hangar_door.png`; before: `work/n4/shots/before_hangar_door_noise.png`, from the S4e build). The 18 areas of the first sweep (`docs/mpr/SWEEP.md`) came from a build made before the alias sharing was merged.
