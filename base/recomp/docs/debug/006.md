# 006 XTN2 cleared the lower half of its destination

**Symptom.** Three tier-1 shapes of `xtn2` failed (36 tests): the upper half was right, the lower half zero.

**Cause.** `v_xtn` in `neon.h` zeroed the whole destination before writing, and had no way to be told it was the "2" form.

**Fix.** `v_xtn(d, n, size, upper)`: the "2" form (Q = 1) writes the upper half and keeps the lower.
